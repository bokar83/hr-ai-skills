# Lessons log, decision record and Friday review

A pilot nobody can repeat is not a result. Write it down so someone else can follow. Use only what the user gives. Write UNKNOWN for missing fields and ask at the end.

## When to use
- End of a work week.
- A real decision was just made.

## When to skip
- There is nothing to record yet.

## Inputs needed
- The user's rough notes for the week.

## Steps
1. LESSONS LOG ENTRY (one per week at most): Entry (week number and short title); Hypothesis (what was expected and what result would prove it wrong); What I did (people named by role, not by name); Measured (use and value, how, how many people); Result (compared with the baseline); Why (best explanation and what else could explain it); What I would change (one thing); Confidence (low, medium or high, and why); Steps to repeat (numbered); Made (template or checklist produced and where it lives); Decision it informed.
2. DECISION RECORD (one per real decision): Decision and date; Options (the two or three that were real); Recommendation and why (the reason in the terms that drove it); Who decided; What would change it; Review date. A decision recorded without its reason is a fossil: the next person will invent a reason, wrongly.
3. Run the 20-minute FRIDAY REVIEW one question at a time, waiting for each answer: (5 min) What did you expect this week and what happened? One line per hypothesis. (5 min) Show the drafted entry and ask for corrections. (5 min) Show the decision records and ask which real decisions are missing. (5 min) What is one thing to try next week and what would tell you it worked?
4. If the user says 'skipped, capacity', write that in the log and stop.

## Quality bar
- Reasons recorded. Roles, not names. Steps repeatable by a stranger.

## Output format
The entry, the decision records, then the review questions.

## If input is missing
Ask for the week's notes.

## Works with
Follows adoption-check-in.

## Rules for every skill
- Use only what the user gave. Do not invent facts, quotes, numbers or dates. Write UNKNOWN or [NEED FACT] instead.
- If you are not sure, say so plainly. Never guess to look confident.
- Plain English. No dashes used as punctuation breaks.
- Never send, post or act on anything. Produce drafts for the user to decide on.
